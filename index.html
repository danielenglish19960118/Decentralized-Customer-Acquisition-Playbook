Decentralized Customer Acquisition Guide

How to build a zero-dependency, censor-resistant marketing pipeline that brings you paying customers without relying on big-tech social media algorithms or rent-seeking platforms.

The Problem: Platform Risk & Algorithm Dependency

If your entire business relies on Facebook Ads, Instagram Reach, Amazon, or YouTube algorithms, you do not own your business—you are renting it.

Arbitrary Banning

One false automated flag can terminate your ad manager or storefront overnight without human review.

Rented Audience

You have to pay again and again to reach followers who have already subscribed to your channel.

High Commissions

Platforms take 15%–30% cuts on transactions while controlling your customer relationship data.

The 4-Layer Acquisition Stack

A resilient pipeline engineered for complete business sovereignty.

Layer 1

Search & Academic Footprint

Establish high-authority open-access previews (5% Free Rule) indexed permanently on GitHub, Zenodo (DOI), and Hugging Face.

Indexable via Google Scholar & Dataset Search

Visual & Content Funnels

Generate 15-second educational video teasers with AI (Runway, Kling) to capture social attention and route traffic to your sovereign domain.

Sovereign Asset Hub
